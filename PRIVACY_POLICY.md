# Privacy Policy

**Grace Pad — Bible Study & Notes App**
**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Grace Pad ("the App"), developed and maintained by Paschal Ozoemen ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Grace Pad mobile application.

By using Grace Pad, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
- **Notes & Content**: The notes, highlights, bookmarks, and scripture annotations you create are stored in your account.
- **Study Progress**: Data about your Bible study plan progress, streaks, and day completions.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: General information about how you interact with the App (e.g. which Bible books you read, which features you use) may be recorded to improve the App.
- **Device Information**: Basic device identifiers, operating system version, and app version may be collected for crash reporting and debugging purposes.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your precise location.
- We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone.
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- We do not serve advertisements inside the App.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- **Provide core functionality**: Sync your notes, bookmarks, highlights, and study progress across sessions.
- **Authenticate your account**: Verify your identity via Google Sign-In.
- **Improve the App**: Understand how users engage with the App to fix bugs and add features.
- **Send notifications**: Deliver devotional and study reminders if you have enabled them (these are local device notifications and do not require a server).
- **Provide AI Study Guides**: When you request a study guide, the topic/chapter is sent to an AI service to generate content. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Where Your Data Is Stored
Your notes and account data are stored in **Google Firebase Firestore**, a secure cloud database. Firebase applies industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

### 4.2 Firestore Security Rules
Your notes are private to your account. Our Firestore security rules ensure:
- Only you can read or write your own notes.
- No other user can access your personal data.

### 4.3 Local Storage
Some data (such as Bible reading progress, verse highlights, and bookmarks) is also stored locally on your device using **AsyncStorage** so the App works offline.

### 4.4 Data Retention
Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

Grace Pad is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
